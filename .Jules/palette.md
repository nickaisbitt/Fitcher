## 2024-05-18 - [Empty States and A11y Visibility]
**Learning:** Adding empty states to data-heavy components (like the Trades Tab) significantly improves the UX by guiding the user to actions (like Backtesting). Ensuring focus visibility via `focus-visible:ring-*` enhances keyboard navigation without disrupting the mouse experience.
**Action:** Continuously review empty tabs/data tables across the platform for similar opportunities and ensure all interactive elements maintain distinct focus states.
