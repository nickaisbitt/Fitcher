## 2024-05-03 - Performance Learnings
**Learning:** O(n^2) bottlenecks and unnecessary memory operations found in hot loops and metrics/stats calculations. Arrays' `shift()` on large history buffers can be slow due to reindexing. `reduce()` arrays without initial accumulators might be slower.
**Action:** Replace `Array.shift()` and `Array.splice()` from the front of arrays with ring buffers or index pointers where possible, especially in `trimOldData` functions in `metricsCollector.js`, `history.shift()` in `DynamicRiskManager.js`, `recentSignals.shift()` in `SignalAggregator.js` and `openBuys.shift()` in `backtestEngine.js`. Replace `.reduce()` and `.filter()` `.map()` chains with single-pass `for` loops in performance-critical areas like `metricsCollector.js` and `backtestEngine.js`.
